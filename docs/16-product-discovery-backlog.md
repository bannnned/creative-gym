## Near-Term Candidates

### Reflection After Photo Comparison

During anonymous comparison, the user compares 3-5 pairs of photographs.
After some choices, the app asks one short optional question:

> Что сделало этот кадр сильнее?

Possible answers:

- свет;
- композиция;
- идея;
- настроение;
- момент;
- неожиданность.

The question should not appear after every pair. It must keep comparison quick
and should feel like a moment of reflection rather than a voting form.

The first experiment can ask the question once during a set of 3-5 pairs. The
answer may be used to show a small personal observation after the set, for
example:

> Сегодня твоё внимание чаще привлекали свет и настроение.

This is not a psychological profile and there are no correct answers. The
purpose is to train visual awareness and give the user an immediate personal
outcome from comparison.

Open questions:

- whether one question per set is enough;
- whether the answer choices should depend on the current workout;
- whether users should be able to skip the question;
- how many choices are needed before a useful observation can be shown;
- whether Coach Notes should appear after selected pairs.

## Ideas To Explore

### Short Visual Warm-Up

Before a workout, show a short interaction built around 3-5 photographs or
photo pairs. The exact format is intentionally undecided. It should:

- take no more than 1-2 minutes;
- help the user notice something about photography;
- provide value immediately;
- introduce the comparison mechanic without presenting a right answer;
- avoid looking like a test, feed, or popularity vote.

### Evergreen First Workout

Offer a simple first assignment that can be completed anywhere without waiting
for a live room to finish. The user should receive an honest immediate outcome
after submitting a photo. The exact outcome and interaction format still need
product exploration; fake votes, fake participants, and artificial ranking
must not be used.
